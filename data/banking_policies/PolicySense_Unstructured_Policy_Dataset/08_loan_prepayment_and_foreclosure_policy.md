# Loan Part-Payment and Foreclosure Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-LOAN-008  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Scope
This policy describes requests to pay part of a loan principal early or close a loan before its scheduled end date. The customer's signed loan agreement and applicable product schedule determine whether a request is permitted and whether a charge applies.

## 2. Part-payment
A part-payment is an additional payment toward the loan before the normal repayment schedule requires it. Depending on the loan terms and the bank's processing rules, the payment may reduce the outstanding principal, affect the remaining tenure, or change later installments. Support must not promise a specific effect before checking the loan product and the customer's request.

## 3. Foreclosure request
Foreclosure means settling the loan in full before the scheduled end date. The customer should request an official foreclosure statement showing the amount required and the validity date of that statement. The amount may depend on outstanding principal, accrued interest, unpaid charges, and any permitted foreclosure fee.

## 4. Why a quoted amount may change
A previously quoted settlement amount may differ from the final amount if additional interest accrues, a payment posts, a statement expires, or another amount becomes due. Support should compare the quote date, validity date, and payment ledger before describing the difference.

## 5. Charges and eligibility
Any part-payment or foreclosure fee must be supported by the customer's loan agreement and disclosed schedule. If the relevant terms are unavailable, the chatbot should say that the fee requires account-specific confirmation rather than inventing a percentage or amount.

## 6. Processing and confirmation
The customer should use the official payment method and retain the reference. A loan should not be described as closed merely because the customer initiated a transfer. Closure is confirmed only after the bank reconciles the payment and issues the appropriate confirmation.

## 7. Disputes
If the customer believes a foreclosure quote, charge, or remaining balance is incorrect, route the case to the loan-support team with the quote date and payment reference. Escalate unresolved matters through the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy.
