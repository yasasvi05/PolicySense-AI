# Refunds, Returns, and Reversals Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-REF-014  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Distinguishing the terms
A **refund** is generally initiated by a merchant or service provider to return a payment. A **reversal** returns or offsets a debit after a transaction fails or cannot be completed. A **chargeback or transaction dispute** is a formal process to investigate a contested payment. The correct process depends on the payment method and transaction status.

## 2. Refund not received
If a customer says a merchant issued a refund that has not appeared, collect the merchant, amount, date, and refund reference if available. Check whether the refund was actually initiated and whether the bank has received or posted it. A merchant's statement that a refund was processed is useful evidence but may not prove that the bank has received the funds.

## 3. Failed payment reversal
For a failed payment with a debit, follow the Digital Payments and Transaction Disputes Policy. Under that fictional policy, the demonstration target is 5 business days from the transaction date after a failure is confirmed. If the target has passed, register a dispute. This target is synthetic and is not a real regulatory timeline.

## 4. Cancelled purchase
A cancelled purchase may show a pending authorization, a completed debit, or a later refund depending on how the merchant and payment network processed it. Support should verify the status rather than treating every cancellation as an automatic immediate refund.

## 5. Refund amount differs
If the credited refund differs from the purchase amount, compare the original transaction, merchant refund record, currency or conversion details if relevant, and any separately posted charge. Do not invent an explanation when these records are unavailable.

## 6. Required next steps
Explain which party must take the next action based on the verified status. If the merchant has not initiated a refund, the customer may need to contact the merchant. If the refund was initiated but not received, the bank may need to trace it. If a debit is unrecognized, follow the Account Security and Unauthorized Transactions Policy.

## 7. Escalation
If the refund or reversal remains unresolved, create a case and provide a reference. Unresolved complaints follow the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy.
