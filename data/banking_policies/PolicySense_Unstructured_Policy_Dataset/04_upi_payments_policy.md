# UPI Payments Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-UPI-004  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to UPI transfers and merchant payments initiated through a supported banking or payment application. It supplements the Digital Payments and Transaction Disputes Policy.

## 2. Payment failed but account debited
A UPI payment may show as failed even though a debit appears in the account when the payment flow is interrupted, the recipient-side confirmation is delayed, or the displayed status has not refreshed. These are possible explanations only. The bank must verify the transaction reference and payment status before stating what happened.

### Required support steps
1. Ask the customer to open the official transaction history and locate the transaction reference.
2. Check the transaction's current status through the approved support process.
3. If the transaction is pending, explain that the outcome has not yet been confirmed.
4. If it is confirmed failed, follow the applicable reversal process and timeline in the Digital Payments and Transaction Disputes Policy.
5. If the reversal target has passed, register a dispute and provide a case reference.

## 3. Payment marked successful but recipient has not received funds
A successful status should be verified against the payment record. Confirm the recipient's UPI ID or merchant details without asking the customer to expose credentials. If the bank's record confirms success, initiate a trace or dispute according to the applicable payment workflow. Do not claim the recipient's bank caused the issue unless the trace supports that conclusion.

## 4. Pending UPI payment
A pending transaction is not the same as a failed transaction. Advise the customer not to repeat the payment immediately if doing so could create a second debit. Check the status and provide the next step according to the payment record. If the customer has already retried and sees multiple debits, use the duplicate-debit procedure.

## 5. Incorrect recipient details
If a customer entered the wrong recipient details, support should collect the transaction reference and route the request for review. Recovery is not guaranteed and depends on transaction status and the applicable process. The bot must not promise that a completed transfer can always be cancelled.

## 6. Safety
The bank will never need the customer's UPI PIN or OTP to issue a refund. Never advise the customer to share or enter a UPI PIN to receive money. Suspected fraud must be reported promptly through official channels.
