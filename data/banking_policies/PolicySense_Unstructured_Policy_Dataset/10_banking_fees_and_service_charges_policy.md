# Banking Fees and Service Charges Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-FEE-010  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. General rule
Fees and service charges depend on the customer's account or product schedule, the service requested, and the terms in force when the event occurred. This document describes the review process but does not establish a universal charge amount.

## 2. Charge categories
Depending on the product schedule, a charge may relate to account maintenance, a replacement card, a loan-related service, a payment service, a returned payment, or another disclosed service. A category listed here does not mean that the charge applies to every customer.

## 3. Customer disputes a fee
When a customer says a fee is incorrect, support should:
1. Identify the transaction description, amount, and posting date.
2. Identify the account or product plan.
3. Retrieve the relevant fee schedule and version.
4. Check whether the triggering event occurred.
5. Explain the basis of the charge or send it for review if the evidence is incomplete.

## 4. Waiver or refund
A waiver or refund may be considered only under the applicable product terms and the bank's approval process. A support conversation or complaint alone does not guarantee approval. The chatbot should not promise a waiver before the authorized review is complete.

## 5. Fee schedule unavailable
If the current or historical fee schedule cannot be found, the chatbot must clearly state that the charge cannot yet be confirmed. It should direct the case to a staff member who can retrieve the correct schedule rather than generating a fee amount.

## 6. Changes to fees
A fee change must be supported by the applicable approved notice and effective date. When a customer disputes the timing of a charge, compare the posting date with the schedule version and notice that applied to the product.

## 7. Escalation
If a fee remains unexplained after checking the product schedule, provide a case reference and route the matter to the relevant account, card, or loan team. Unresolved complaints follow the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy.
