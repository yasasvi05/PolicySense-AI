# Credit Card Billing and Disputes Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-CC-006  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Billing cycle and statement
A credit-card statement summarizes posted transactions, fees, interest, payments, and the amount due for a billing period. Pending authorizations may not appear as final transactions. Customers should compare the statement date, transaction date, merchant, and amount before raising a billing question.

## 2. Total amount due and minimum amount due
The total amount due is the full statement balance requested by the statement. The minimum amount due is the minimum payment specified on that statement. Paying only the minimum may leave a balance subject to interest and may affect the interest-free period under the card terms. The chatbot should use the customer's actual statement to explain their amount; it must not calculate a personalized amount without the required data.

## 3. Late payment
A late-payment fee or interest may apply when the required payment is not received by the due date, subject to the card agreement and disclosed fee schedule. Before explaining a charge, verify the due date, payment date, posting date, and applicable card terms. A payment initiated before the due date may not be posted immediately; the bank should check its recorded receipt and posting details.

## 4. Unrecognized transaction
If the customer does not recognize a card transaction, advise them to report it through the secure official channel promptly. The bank may secure the card and investigate authorization and merchant records. Do not state that the transaction is fraudulent until the investigation supports that finding.

## 5. Duplicate transaction or incorrect amount
The customer should provide the merchant, date, amount, and transaction reference. The bank will determine whether the entries are pending authorizations, separate purchases, a duplicate posting, or a merchant adjustment. A refund is not guaranteed until the facts are confirmed.

## 6. Payment not reflected
If a card payment is not reflected, check the payment method, reference, initiation date, and statement balance. A delay between initiation and posting may explain the difference, but support must verify the payment record before confirming. The customer should avoid making an unnecessary duplicate payment without first checking the status.

## 7. Fee review and complaints
If the customer believes a fee or interest charge is incorrect, request a statement review. Any waiver is subject to the bank's approved authority and is not guaranteed by this policy. Escalate unresolved cases under the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy.
