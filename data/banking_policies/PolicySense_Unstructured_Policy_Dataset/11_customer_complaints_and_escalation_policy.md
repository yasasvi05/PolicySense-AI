# Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-COMP-011  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how customers can raise a complaint, how a case should be recorded, and how unresolved issues are escalated within the fictional bank.

## 2. What counts as a complaint
A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction about a product, service, transaction, fee, delay, staff interaction, or the handling of a prior request. A customer does not need to use the word “complaint” for the issue to be recorded as one.

## 3. Information to record
Collect only information needed to identify and investigate the issue, such as:
- A short description of the problem
- Relevant date and amount
- Transaction or case reference, if available
- Product category
- Preferred approved contact channel

Do not collect passwords, PINs, OTPs, full card security codes, or unnecessary identity information in ordinary chat.

## 4. Acknowledgment and updates
Under this fictional demonstration policy, the bank aims to acknowledge a registered complaint within 2 business days and provide a meaningful status update within 7 business days. These are invented service targets for the dataset and must not be represented as real-world or regulatory timelines. If investigation will take longer, the assigned team should explain that further review is required and provide an update channel.

## 5. Escalation stages
**Stage 1 — Service team:** First review of the issue and collection of relevant details.

**Stage 2 — Specialist team:** Payments, cards, deposits, accounts, or loans team investigates cases requiring product-specific access.

**Stage 3 — Grievance review:** If the customer disagrees with the resolution or the case remains unresolved, route it to the designated grievance-review channel.

## 6. Communicating a decision
A response should explain what was checked, what was found, what action will be taken, and what the customer can do if they disagree. Do not say a case has been resolved until the relevant action is complete and recorded.

## 7. Policy conflict or missing information
If two documents appear inconsistent, or the required product schedule is unavailable, the chatbot should disclose that it cannot determine the final answer from the available policy text and route the case for review. It must not silently choose a rule or fabricate a resolution.
