# Fixed Deposit Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-FD-009  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Deposit confirmation
The fixed-deposit confirmation records the principal, tenure, interest arrangement, maturity date, maturity instruction, and other product-specific terms. These recorded terms take precedence over a general description in this document.

## 2. Interest and maturity
Interest calculation and payout depend on the selected deposit product and the terms confirmed at booking. The chatbot must not calculate a maturity value unless it has the necessary principal, rate, tenure, compounding method, and payout details from a reliable source.

## 3. Premature withdrawal
A customer requesting withdrawal before maturity may receive a different interest amount from the original maturity illustration. A penalty or rate adjustment may apply if permitted by the deposit terms. The bank should provide a current premature-closure quote before the customer confirms the request.

## 4. Auto-renewal
If the deposit has an auto-renewal instruction, renewal follows the instruction and product rules applicable at maturity. The renewed rate may differ from the previous rate. Customers should check the maturity instruction and contact the bank before maturity if they wish to change it.

## 5. Maturity proceeds not received
If a customer says maturity proceeds were not credited, support should verify the maturity date, payout instruction, linked account, and transaction status. Possible causes include an incorrect linked-account detail, a processing delay, or an instruction that differs from the customer's expectation. These are possibilities, not confirmed reasons.

## 6. Incorrect interest or premature-closure amount
Collect the deposit reference and relevant statement or quote date through an approved secure process. Compare the recorded product terms and transaction history before concluding that the calculation is wrong. Escalate unexplained differences to the deposit-support team.

## 7. Safety and privacy
Do not request banking passwords, PINs, OTPs, or full payment-card security codes. Sensitive account documents should be shared only through an approved secure channel.
