# Account Security and Unauthorized Transactions Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-SEC-012  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers suspected account compromise, phishing, lost payment instruments, and transactions the customer says they did not authorize.

## 2. Immediate customer guidance
If a customer reports a suspicious transaction or believes their account is compromised, advise them to use the bank's official verified channel immediately to secure the affected account or payment instrument. They should review recent activity and report the transaction for investigation. Do not delay urgent security steps while gathering nonessential details.

## 3. Credentials and social engineering
The bank will not ask the customer to disclose their password, PIN, OTP, recovery code, or full card security code in chat. A customer should not install remote-access software or share their screen with an unknown person claiming to be bank support. A refund does not require the customer to reveal a PIN or OTP.

## 4. Suspicious link or impersonation
If the customer clicked a suspicious link or shared information with someone claiming to represent the bank, recommend contacting the official bank channel and reporting what was shared. Do not blame the customer or promise a particular financial outcome before the investigation.

## 5. Unauthorized transaction investigation
The bank must verify transaction details and relevant access records through approved internal systems. A customer's report should be taken seriously, but the chatbot must not label a transaction definitively as fraud before review. It should also avoid revealing internal security controls or fraud-detection signals.

## 6. Card or account blocking
A customer may request blocking of a card or access channel through an approved process. The bot should provide the correct official route when available, but must not claim that a block has been completed unless a connected system confirms the action. If the chatbot cannot perform the action, it should say so clearly.

## 7. Follow-up and dispute
Record the report reference, affected channel, approximate transaction time, and the customer's description, without collecting credentials. Route the case to the security team and provide a case reference. Unresolved complaints follow the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy.
