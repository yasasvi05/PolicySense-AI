# Debit Card Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-CARD-005  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Card activation and usage
A debit card may require activation and setup of permitted usage channels before transactions can be completed. Transaction controls may differ for ATM withdrawals, point-of-sale purchases, online payments, and international usage. The customer should review the controls available in the official banking application.

## 2. Declined transactions
A declined transaction may result from insufficient available balance, a disabled usage channel, a limit, an expired card, incorrect details, a security block, or a merchant-side issue. These are possible causes only. The bank must check the decline reason available in its approved system before giving a definitive explanation.

## 3. Card blocked or retained
A card may be temporarily blocked after repeated incorrect PIN attempts, a suspected security event, a customer request, or an internal review. If an ATM retains a card, the customer should not accept help from unknown individuals nearby and should contact the bank through an official channel. Support must verify the case before promising that a retained card can be returned.

## 4. Lost or stolen card
The customer should immediately use the official application, verified phone channel, or branch process to block a lost or stolen card. The customer should review recent transactions and report any unrecognized activity. Never request a full PIN, OTP, or card security code in chat.

## 5. Duplicate card charge
If a purchase appears twice, compare the merchant name, amount, date, and transaction references. One entry may be an authorization hold while another is the final posting; alternatively, the entries may represent two completed transactions. The bank must verify the posting status before describing the issue as a duplicate charge.

## 6. Replacement and fees
A replacement card may be issued after identity verification. Any replacement fee depends on the customer's disclosed account and card schedule. The bot must not invent a fee amount when the relevant schedule is unavailable.

## 7. Disputes and escalation
For an unrecognized or incorrect card transaction, direct the customer to the secure dispute process. The case should include transaction details but must exclude passwords, PINs, OTPs, and full card security codes. Unresolved matters follow the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy.
