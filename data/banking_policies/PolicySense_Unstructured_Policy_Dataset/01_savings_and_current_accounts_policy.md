# Savings and Current Accounts Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-ACC-001  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy describes account opening, minimum-balance arrangements, inactive accounts, account restrictions, and closure requests for eligible savings and current accounts maintained with Northstar Community Bank.

## 2. Account opening and eligibility
Applicants must complete the bank's identity-verification process and provide the information requested for the selected account type. An application is not considered approved until the bank confirms that verification and internal checks are complete. The bank may request additional documents when submitted information is incomplete or inconsistent.

## 3. Minimum balance
Some account plans may have a minimum average balance requirement; other plans may not. The applicable requirement is the one stated in the customer's account-plan schedule at account opening or in a later approved notice. If a required balance is not maintained, a service charge may apply only where the account plan permits it and the customer has been informed of the applicable charge.

**Example scenario:** A customer sees a maintenance fee and believes it was charged by mistake. The bank should first identify the account plan, review the applicable balance period, and check the plan's disclosed fee schedule. The fee should not be described as correct until those details are verified.

## 4. Inactive and dormant accounts
For this fictional policy, an account with no customer-initiated financial transaction for 12 consecutive months is classified as inactive. An account with no customer-initiated financial transaction for 24 consecutive months may be classified as dormant after the bank's required review and notice process. System-generated interest entries and bank charges alone do not count as customer-initiated transactions for this classification.

An inactive or dormant classification may limit selected transaction channels until the customer completes the bank's reactivation checks. The customer should contact the bank through an approved channel and complete any requested identity checks. Staff must not promise immediate reactivation before the checks are completed.

## 5. Account restrictions
A restriction may be placed when verification is incomplete, suspicious activity is being reviewed, a legal instruction applies, or a material account detail needs confirmation. The reason and permitted next steps depend on the restriction type. The customer-support assistant must not reveal internal fraud-monitoring signals or claim that a restriction is punitive.

## 6. Account closure
Customers may request account closure after settling outstanding dues and arranging transfers of any remaining balance. The bank may require identity verification and return or cancellation of linked payment instruments. Closure may be delayed if a transaction is pending, a legal restriction applies, or required documentation is incomplete. Any closure fee must be supported by the customer's disclosed account schedule.

## 7. Exceptions and escalation
If a customer disputes a fee, restriction, or dormant status, collect the account type and the date or description of the issue. Refer the matter for account-specific review rather than assuming the policy was applied correctly. Unresolved complaints can be escalated under the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy.
