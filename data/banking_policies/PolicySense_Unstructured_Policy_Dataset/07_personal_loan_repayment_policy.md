# Personal Loan Repayment Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-LOAN-007  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Repayment schedule
The loan agreement and repayment schedule specify the installment amount, due date, repayment method, and any applicable charges. The customer's agreement is the primary source for account-specific amounts and dates. A general policy document must not be used to guess a customer's EMI or outstanding balance.

## 2. EMI debit failed
An installment debit may fail because of insufficient funds, an inactive or changed payment mandate, a technical issue, or incorrect account details. These are possible causes, not confirmed findings. Support should verify the mandate status, scheduled date, and debit result before stating a reason.

The customer should check the official loan account and payment method, then follow the bank's approved retry or alternate-payment process. The customer should retain the payment reference for any payment already made.

## 3. Late installment
A late fee or additional interest may apply if an installment is not received as required by the loan agreement. The exact amount and calculation depend on the agreement and disclosed schedule. Before explaining a charge, verify the due date, amount due, amount received, posting date, and applicable terms.

## 4. Payment made but loan account not updated
A payment may take time to appear in the loan account. Support should verify the transaction reference and payment destination. If payment is confirmed but the loan ledger has not updated, create a reconciliation case. Do not tell the customer to pay again until the original payment status has been checked.

## 5. Part-payment and missed-payment questions
Part-payment, installment rescheduling, or changes to a repayment date require review under the customer's loan agreement and the bank's approval process. The bot must not promise that a missed installment can be waived or that a due date can be changed automatically.

## 6. Hardship and disputed charges
Customers who report temporary financial hardship or dispute a charge should be referred to the loan-support team for available options. A request does not guarantee a waiver, restructuring, or change to the agreement.

## 7. Escalation
Cases involving a confirmed payment but an unchanged loan balance, disputed charges, or repeated debit failure should receive a case reference. Unresolved complaints follow the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy.
