# Account Holds and Restricted Transactions Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-HOLD-013  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Scope
This policy explains how support should handle customer questions about a transaction hold, a temporarily restricted account, or a payment that cannot be completed. A hold can have several causes, and the precise reason must be verified in the account record.

## 2. Possible reasons for a hold
A hold may relate to a pending authorization, a verification issue, a review of unusual activity, a legal instruction, or another product-specific process. This list is illustrative, not a determination of why any individual account is restricted.

## 3. Hold versus completed debit
A pending authorization or hold is not necessarily the same as a completed debit. Support should inspect the transaction status, available balance, ledger balance, and relevant reference before explaining the difference. Do not tell the customer that money has been permanently taken if the record only shows a pending hold.

## 4. What the customer can do
The customer should check the official transaction history and any bank notification about the restriction. If additional verification is requested, they should use the approved secure channel. If the hold relates to a merchant authorization, the customer may also need to contact the merchant through a verified channel.

## 5. Release or removal
A hold may be released after the underlying transaction completes, expires, is reversed, or is reviewed. The timing depends on the hold type and the relevant payment or product process. The chatbot must not promise a release time unless a current applicable policy or account-specific system provides one.

## 6. Restricted account
If the account itself is restricted, support should verify the restriction category and the permitted next step. Do not reveal confidential internal review details. If the reason cannot be disclosed or confirmed in the chatbot, route the case to the authorized team.

## 7. Escalation
If a hold persists beyond the applicable timeline or the customer disputes the restriction, open a case with the transaction reference and relevant dates. Follow the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy for unresolved matters.
