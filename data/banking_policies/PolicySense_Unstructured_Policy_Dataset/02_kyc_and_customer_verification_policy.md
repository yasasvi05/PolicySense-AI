# KYC and Customer Verification Policy

**Policy ID:** NCB-KYC-002  
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 1 October 2026  
**Institution:** Northstar Community Bank (fictional)

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out the fictional bank's process for verifying customer identity, updating records, and handling incomplete or inconsistent verification information.

## 2. Verification requirements
Customers must provide the identity and address information requested for their account and customer category. The bank reviews submitted information for completeness and consistency. Additional documents or clarification may be requested if a name, address, date, or document image cannot be verified.

## 3. Periodic updates
The bank may ask a customer to review and update records when information becomes outdated, a document expires, contact details change, or a scheduled review is due. A request should identify the information needing attention and the approved method for submission. Customers should not send identity documents to unofficial email addresses or through unverified links.

## 4. Incomplete verification and account impact
If required verification remains incomplete after notices and applicable review, the bank may limit selected services until the issue is resolved. The exact limitation depends on the reason and account type. A chatbot should explain that incomplete verification is one possible reason only when the account-specific status supports it; it must not infer a KYC failure merely because a transaction was declined.

## 5. Correcting a mismatch
If a customer reports a spelling mismatch, an outdated address, or a rejected document, support should:
1. Identify which record or document was rejected.
2. Explain the accepted submission channel.
3. Tell the customer what information must match.
4. Request review if the customer believes the rejection is incorrect.
5. Avoid collecting full identity numbers or sensitive documents in an ordinary chat.

## 6. Security and privacy
Support staff must not ask customers to disclose passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes, full card security codes, or online-banking credentials. A request for verification must use an approved secure channel.

## 7. Review and escalation
A customer who believes an account restriction was caused by an incorrect verification decision may request a manual review. If unresolved, the case follows the Customer Complaints and Escalation Policy. All timelines and required documents in this document are fictional examples and must be replaced before real use.
