HEALTH SECURE PLUS - POLICY TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Version 3.2 (synthetic demo document for prototype use)

Section 3.1 - Coverage
The policy covers in-patient hospitalisation of 24 hours or more, day-care procedures, pre-hospitalisation expenses for 30 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days, ambulance charges up to Rs 3,000 per event, and one annual health check-up up to the limit stated in the policy schedule.

Section 4.3 - Waiting Periods
Pre-existing conditions are covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage. Specified illnesses such as cataract, hernia and joint replacement carry a waiting period of 12 months from the policy start date.

Section 6.1 - Premium Payment
Premium must be paid on or before the due date shown in the policy schedule. Payment can be made by UPI, net banking, debit or credit card, or auto-debit mandate.

Section 6.2 - Late Payment Clause
If the renewal premium is received after the due date but within the 30-day grace period, a late payment fee is added to the premium payable. A late fee of Rs 500 applies when payment is received 1 to 15 days after the due date. A late fee of Rs 1,000 applies when payment is received 16 to 30 days after the due date. The late fee is charged once per renewal cycle and is non-refundable.

Section 7.1 - Renewal and Grace Period
The policy must be renewed on or before the renewal due date. A grace period of 30 days is allowed after the due date, during which no claims are payable. If the policy is not renewed within the grace period it lapses, and continuity benefits such as waiting-period credits and the no-claim bonus are lost. A lapsed policy can only be revived by fresh underwriting.

Section 8.1 - Exclusions
The following are not covered: cosmetic or plastic surgery unless needed after an accident or for cancer treatment, dental treatment unless caused by an accident, infertility and assisted reproduction, self-inflicted injury, injury arising from war, and treatment taken outside India unless the plan states otherwise.

Section 9.1 - Claim Procedure
Planned hospitalisation must be intimated at least 3 days in advance and emergency hospitalisation within 48 hours of admission. Claim documents must be submitted within 15 days of discharge. A claim may be rejected if the treatment is an excluded procedure, falls within a waiting period, has incomplete documentation, or involves non-disclosure of material facts.
