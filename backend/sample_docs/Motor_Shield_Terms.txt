Section 2.1 - Own Damage Cover
Loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accident, fire, theft, natural calamities and riots is covered up to the insured declared value.

Section 5.1 - Premium and Late Payment
Premium is due on the renewal due date. If the premium is received after the due date, a late fee of Rs 300 is added for delays up to 15 days.

Section 5.2 - Renewal and No-Claim Bonus
The policy should be renewed before expiry. If renewal is delayed by more than 90 days, the accumulated no-claim bonus is forfeited and a vehicle inspection is mandatory.

Section 6.1 - Claim Procedure
Claims must be intimated within 24 hours of the incident. An FIR is required for theft and third-party injury. Claims may be rejected for drunk driving, driving without a valid licence, or use of the vehicle outside the permitted purpose.
